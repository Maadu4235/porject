Our Mission

Dwar, meaning "Doorway" in Hindi, is your gateway to trusted local opportunities in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities across India.

We're on a mission to bridge the information gap that hides opportunities in plain sight, creating a discovery layer that connects communities without relying on guesswork, informal networks, or slow channels.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Trust & Verification

Every listing undergoes multiple verification checks to ensure authenticity. We believe trust is the foundation of meaningful connections.

Community First

Built for and by the community. We listen, adapt, and grow with the people we serve, ensuring our solution meets real local needs.

Accessibility

Designed to work on low-end devices and in areas with limited internet connectivity. Everyone deserves access to opportunities.
